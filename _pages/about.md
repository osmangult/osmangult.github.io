Dr. Gültekin, the PI of the Computational Solid Mechanics Laboratory, joined the Deparment of Civil Engineering  after nearly a 15-year break in April 2026. Before joining METU as a faculty, he had received his academic training at [TU Dresden](https://tu-dresden.de/), University of Texas at Austin, Mechanical Engineering at METU, TU Graz, and University of Stuttgart. They add up to four different countries. See `img\` folder. 

He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering from METU and a Master’s in Computational Mechanics of Materials and Structures (COMMAS) from the University of Stuttgart, where he focused on phase-field modeling of fracture in anisotropic media. Later, he pursued his PhD at Graz University of Technology under the supervision of Dr. Gerhard A. Holzapfel, specializing in computational modeling of soft biological tissues, including anisotropic damage and fracture modeling in arterial walls in health and disease. Between 2019 and 2022, he worked as a postdoctoral researcher
at METU ME, contributing to two independent research projects on thermo-viscoelastic behavior and thermomechanical fracture in polymers, one of which was funded by the EU’s Horizon
2020 MSCA Cofund program. He also held research positions at the University of Texas at Austin between 2022-2023, where he worked on clot fracture and Graph Neural Network modeling of heart
valves, and at TU Dresden between 2023-2025, focusing on physics-informed neural networks for soft tissue modeling and participating in an industrial project on viscoelastic friction prediction.
