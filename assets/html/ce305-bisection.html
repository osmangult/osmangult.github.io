Bisection method

Find two points where f(x) has opposite signs. Cut the interval in half, keep the half where the sign still changes, and repeat.

How fast does the error drop?

Bisection halves the bracket every step, so the error falls by a fixed factor: roughly a straight line here. That is linear convergence.
